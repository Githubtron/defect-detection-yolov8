01 System Manifesto / DV-SYS-001

Machine vision
does not blink.

Human inspectors are good. They are also tired at hour seven, distracted at hour nine, and absent at 3 AM. DefectVision watches every part, every frame, at the same clinical intensity — forever.

One vision pipeline handles stills, batches, and full walkaround video. Every detection carries a class, a confidence score, and coordinates — evidence, not opinion.
